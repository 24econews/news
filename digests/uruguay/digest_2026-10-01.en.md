Fitch Ratings, for its part, maintained Uruguay's investment grade but issued an explicit warning about low growth and rising public debt — echoing concerns the Consejo Fiscal Autónomo raised before Parliament when it questioned an "overestimation" in the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas' projections included in the Rendición de Cuentas.
