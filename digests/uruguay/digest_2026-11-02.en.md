The combination of both pieces of news precisely illustrates the structural tension defining the country's economic moment: Uruguay retains its institutional credibility with multilateral organizations and international markets, but has failed to translate that reputation into real dynamism.

According to sources at Cadena del Mar and UY Press, the data cast doubt on growth projections for the full year — a scenario that Economy Minister Gabriel Oddone himself has anticipated with uncommon candor for a sitting official: "There is a fairly high probability that we will revise downward the growth projected for 2026," he recently stated, according to La Diaria.
