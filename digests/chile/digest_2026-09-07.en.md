The paradox is stark: while July's Imacec fell 1.5% month-on-month — dragging annual growth below 1% — and second-quarter GDP contracted 0.2%, the pipeline of future investment has never been fuller. But analysts warn that the deterioration of the labor market and household expectations are amplifying the drag on consumption and services, creating a short-term negative loop that coexists with structurally positive signals.
