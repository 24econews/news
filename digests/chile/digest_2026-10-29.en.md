Finance Minister Jorge Quiroz was unusually blunt before more than twelve hundred attendees at the Metropolitan Santiago: "We are going to be living with negative Imacec figures in some months for the remainder of the year," attributing part of the deterioration to drops of 15% to 17% in copper mining output in recent months. The divergence between mining Chile and services Chile is today the most important tension framing the country's economic debate.

The consensus at Enade was that the economy will grow more than 3% in 2027, a figure that appears in the National Budget and that the CPC, led by Susana Jiménez, also endorses, projecting a range of between 3% and 3.5%.
