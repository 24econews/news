Decree 690/2026, signed by Deregulation Minister Federico Sturzenegger to open pilotage services to market competition, triggered an indefinite work stoppage by pilots, harbor pilots, and river guides that has paralyzed foreign trade, delayed grain and fuel shipments, and even blocked a semi-submersible vessel bound for the Vaca Muerta Oil Sur project.
