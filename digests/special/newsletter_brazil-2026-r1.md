# Brazil's runoff: eight columnists on what the first round means for the economy

This special edition brings together eight columns, each from a different 24EcoNews columnist, on what Brazil's October 4 first-round election result means for the economy. Together, they examine what the October 25 runoff could still change — from fiscal policy and social spending to trade, geopolitics, and the country's broader economic direction.

Whoever you are, a start-up founder in São Paulo, an economist working near 19th and H in Washington, a policy analyst in Brussels or Beijing, a sustainable fund manager in Rio, there is something for you in these eight columns. They disagree with one another on purpose, so read the ones that challenge you most. If they're useful, forward this edition to colleagues and friends who follow Brazil. And tell us what you think: write to the editor at contact-the-team@24econews.com. We may publish a selection of letters on the site, with the writer's permission.

— John Dominguez, Editor
